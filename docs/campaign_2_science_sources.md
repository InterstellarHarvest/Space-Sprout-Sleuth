# Campaign 2 science-source register

This register documents the external science used to bound the game content. It is not a curriculum package or worksheet. Alien species, exact alien response thresholds, fictional institutions, and narrative outcomes remain inventions.

## Case 01 — Heavy Hands

- Established model: rotational acceleration follows \(a=\omega^2r\). NASA's [Development and Comparison of an Artificial Gravity Concept for Human Spaceflight](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/20205004959) states the same radius/angular-speed relationship.
- Runtime model: 2.10 g at a 224.9 m midpoint implies 2.8896592893 RPM. A 0.2 m radial bed spans 2.0990662517 g to 2.1009337483 g, a 0.0018674967 g magnitude difference. Direction remains outward along the same radial line.
- Fiction boundary: gorlroot's ability to detect and overcorrect for that small difference is invented. The evidence supports only the scenario diagnosis and a controlled cultivation trial.

## Case 02 — The Missing Dance

- Established model: poricidal anthers have existing pores, and bee-generated mechanical vibration transmits through floral tissue. Pollen release depends on vibration amplitude and other mechanical properties, not a single universal frequency: [Biomechanical properties of a buzz-pollinated flower](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7540744/), [Transmission of bee-like vibrations](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8241880/), and [Carpenter bee thorax vibration and force generation](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9355986/).
- Fiction boundary: a hovering alien moth coupling airborne pressure oscillations into a flexible cone, with the strongest response near 124 Hz, is explicit fiction. Frequency, amplitude, duration, and coupling all remain variables in the proposed trial.

## Case 03 — The Wrong Color of Light

- Established model: brown algae contain chlorophyll a/c and fucoxanthin light-harvesting complexes; energy transfer among those pigments is experimentally characterized in [Light-harvesting systems of brown algae and diatoms](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/7016188/).
- Fiction boundary: zhal-kelp's accessory pigments, 460–540 nm scenario band, habitat profile, and comparative efficiency are fictional measurements. The game does not claim a universal action spectrum or complete disappearance of red light at a fixed ocean depth.

## Case 04 — The Silent Grove

- Established model: clocks can be entrained by environmental cycles, and continuous-light responses differ among plants. The cross-species experiment [Diel time-courses of leaf growth](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2852670/) found different persistence of rhythmic growth among species under continuous light; [Plants under continuous light](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21396878/) reviews both injury and tolerance.
- Fiction boundary: the Zhel'ii VOC pathway, five-hour minimum dark interval, 7–10 day suppression window, and recovery mechanism are alien records. They are supported in-game by the before/after timeline, not by universal claims about Earth stomata, defense, or night metabolism.

## Case 05 — Too Clean

- Established model: absorbed dose is energy per unit mass and is measured in gray; equivalent/effective dose in sievert adds radiation and tissue weighting. See the IAEA's [Radiation Biology handbook](https://www-pub.iaea.org/MTCD/Publications/PDF/TCS-42_web.pdf).
- Earth analogy boundary: the experiment [Ionizing Radiation Changes the Electronic Properties of Melanin and Enhances the Growth of Melanized Fungi](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0000457) reports particular laboratory effects. It does not establish radiation-powered photosynthesis or a radiation requirement.
- Fiction and safety boundary: karreth/Rhessi pathways and dose rates are fictional. The runtime uses plant-tissue absorbed-dose rate in mGy/day and “below detection,” not absolute zero. Any intervention requires licensed radiation-protection and radiological-engineering teams, characterization, shielding, interlocks, dosimetry, controls, monitoring, and stop criteria. No isotope or operating recipe is supplied.

## Case 06 — The First Garden

- Established model: mycorrhizal fungi can colonize roots, extend hyphae into soil, exchange resources with hosts, and sometimes connect multiple plants.
- Uncertainty boundary: the evidence does not justify a universal cooperative “wood wide web.” [Positive citation bias and overinterpreted results](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41559-023-01986-1) audits common claims; [Arbuscular mycorrhizal fungi influence host infection](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9827988/) demonstrates that inoculation can carry both benefits and risks; [Inoculation effects spread beyond directly inoculated plants](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5524347/) supports explicit monitoring for spread.
- Runtime decision boundary: missing compatible partners are a supported candidate cause, not proven history. No option rewards unauthorized inoculation. Every path requires organism identification, provenance and pathogen screening, host-compatibility checks, approval, controls, monitoring, and staged expansion only after evidence.
- Quantitative context used only in the optional fact panel: [Global density and biomass of arbuscular mycorrhizal fungal networks](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.adu4373) estimates global topsoil hyphal length; [Mycorrhizal mycelium as a global carbon pool](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0960982223001677) estimates annual plant-carbon allocation to mycorrhizal mycelium. The latter is a flux estimate, not permanent storage.

## Instructional use

Each case deliberately distinguishes three layers:

1. established Earth science used as an analogy or quantitative tool;
2. measured evidence that is true inside the fictional case;
3. uncertainty that requires a controlled test rather than a guaranteed remedy.

The sources constrain game claims. They do not preapprove standards alignment, instructional architecture, worksheets, curriculum packages, or production readiness.
